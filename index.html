Scoring Rules
The player with the highest score wins!
  • For each square in the player's remaining pieces, the player gets -1 points.
  • Finish Bonus: If a player places all 21 pieces on the board, the player gets +15 points.
  • Monomino Bonus: If a player places all 21 pieces and the piece placed was the smallest piece (single-square), the player gets an additional +5 points.
Tiebraker rule:
  • If multiple players have the same score count, the winner is the one who played the smaller last piece.
  • If multiple players have the same score count and completed the game by placing all 21 pieces, the winner is the one who placed the single-square piece last.
